# Wind tunnel: Rosewood (Heeled ankle boot)

**Predicted return rate, original page:** 50% ± 25%
**After fixes:** 0% ± 5%

## Weaknesses found

1. **The page says width is "Regular" and gives no fit guidance. It doesn't say the boot is true to size or that the toe box is narrow, so wide-footed shoppers have no cue to size up.** (4 shoppers) — Size 6 wide-footed shopper bought her usual 6 and is a likely "Too small" return: "The page says the width is 'Regular' and doesn't say how the boots fit, so I'm a bit unsure about my wide feet." Three other Rosewood shoppers also asked whether it runs true to size.
2. **The Rosewood photo and swatch show bright rosy pink. The real boot is a deep red-brown with a slight purple cast, darker than on most screens. The page has no colour description or note on this.** (4 shoppers) — All four Rosewood shoppers bought it expecting pink. The colour-particular size 7 shopper, who "buys what the photo shows," is a likely "Colour" return: "The Rosewood pink suede is exactly what I saw in the ad."

## Suggested fixes (need merchandiser approval)

- **Fit note:** True to size with a narrow toe box. Take your usual size if your feet are narrow or regular. If your feet are wide, consider going up half a size.
- **Colour note:** Rosewood is a deep red-brown with a slight purple cast. It is darker than it looks on most screens and is not bright pink.
- **Photo shot list:** Reshoot Rosewood in natural daylight with no colour filter or heavy retouching, and match the swatch and ad images to the real boot colour. Shots needed: front three-quarter, side profile showing the 4 cm stacked heel and side zip, top-down view of the almond toe to show how narrow it is, a close-up of the suede nap, and an on-foot shot. Add Rosewood next to Black for colour reference. Check the final images against the physical boot on a calibrated screen and a phone.
- **Feed facts:** `{"width": "narrow", "heel_cm": 4.0, "colour_text": "Deep red-brown with a slight purple cast; darker than it appears on most screens"}`

## Site-wide weaknesses

- Product pages carry no size or fit guidance (true to size, runs small or large). Shoppers on every product asked "does it run true?" and kept their usual size. On Belide that led to a likely "Too big" return.
- There is no size guide, and "Width: Regular" is the only width information. Wide-footed and narrow-footed shoppers can't tell whether a boot's toe box or heel will suit them.
- Pages give no comfort construction details such as lining, insole or cushioning, sole type, or weight. Shoppers who stand for long periods (a nurse on 12-hour shifts, a wedding guest, an evening-out shopper) asked for these. State them only where they are known.
- Care instructions for suede and customer reviews are missing.
- Ad imagery and swatch colours aren't checked against the real product colour. Rosewood shows the gap, so other colourways should be checked too.
